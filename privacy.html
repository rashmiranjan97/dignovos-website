Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Applies to the Dignovos Android app (com.dignovos.app) and this website.

In short: Dignovos does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. The app has no internet permission. All diagnostic results stay on your device unless you choose to share a report yourself.

1. Data we collect

We don't collect any data. The app has no account or sign-in, and no analytics, advertising or crash reporting services. It doesn't request Android's internet permission, so it can't send data to us or to anyone else.

2. Information the app reads on your device

To run diagnostics, the app reads technical information from Android. This information is processed only on your device:

  • Device details: manufacturer, model, Android version, security patch level, processor, memory, storage and display specifications.
  • Battery status: level, temperature, voltage, charging state and the health status Android reports.
  • Sensor readings and hardware availability: for example accelerometer, gyroscope, NFC, Bluetooth and SIM state.
  • Network status: whether Wi-Fi or mobile data is connected, signal strength, link speed and band. The app does not read Wi-Fi network names, IP or MAC addresses, or your phone number.

The app doesn't read your IMEI or serial number, because Android restricts both. If you choose to type your IMEI into the app, it's used only to display it in your reports and to check that it's in a valid format. It is never sent anywhere or logged.

3. Permissions

Dignovos asks for each permission only when you start the test that needs it, never when the app opens. You can refuse any of them and the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Camera: camera test. Preview images and test photos are kept in memory only and are discarded when you leave the test. Nothing is saved to your gallery or storage.
  • Microphone: microphone test. The short recording is kept in memory only, played back to you, and then discarded.
  • Location: GPS test. It checks satellite reception for up to 45 seconds. Your location is not stored, displayed on a map or shared.
  • Other permissions (network state, Wi-Fi state, vibration, audio settings, biometrics, Bluetooth on older Android versions): these are granted automatically by Android and used only to read hardware status or run the related test. The biometrics check only asks Android whether a fingerprint or face sensor is available. It never asks you to scan your fingerprint or face.

When you leave a test, the app immediately stops using the camera, microphone, location and sensors. You can revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Dignovos › Permissions.

4. Inspection reports

When you save an inspection, the report is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The app keeps up to 50 reports. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. You can delete individual reports in the app, or delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

If you share a report as text or a PDF, Android's share menu sends it to the app or person you choose. What happens to it after that is controlled by that app or recipient, not by Dignovos. PDF files created for sharing are kept in the app's temporary cache.

The storage test writes a 16 MB temporary file with no personal content and deletes it straight away.

5. Children

Dignovos is a general-purpose utility and isn't directed at children. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

6. This website

dignovos.com uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operations.

7. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. Dignovos is distributed through Google Play, and Google's own privacy policy covers your use of the Play Store.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data differently, such as the optional cloud backup we're considering, we'll update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above. Any such feature will be optional and need your consent.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@dignovos.com.
