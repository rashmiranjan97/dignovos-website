  • Only tests you actually ran are counted. Skipped tests are never treated as passed.
  • A single serious problem caps that area's score, so it can't be hidden by an average.

The inspection report

Each report includes:

  • An overall score, and a score for each area
  • Problems found, and what's working well
  • Which tests were not run, and which aren't supported on this phone
  • An A–D condition grade, kept separate from the score
  • A unique inspection ID and the date
  • Export as a PDF or share as text. Up to 50 reports are kept on your phone.
Honest by design

We never guess, and we never make up a number.

Android limits what apps can measure. Some diagnostic apps fill those gaps with estimates. Dignovos tells you when Android doesn't share something.

Privacy

Your phone's data stays on your phone.

Dignovos doesn't even ask for internet access. Nothing it measures can leave your device unless you choose to share a report.

Read the privacy policy
  • No account, no sign-in. Open the app and start.
  • No internet permission. No analytics, ads or crash reporting services.
  • Permissions only when needed. The camera, microphone and location are requested only when you start the test that uses them.
  • Nothing saved without you. Test photos and recordings stay in memory only and are discarded when you leave the test.
  • Reports stay on your phone. They are excluded from cloud backup, and you can delete them at any time.
FAQ

Common questions

Is Dignovos free?

Yes. All the diagnostics in this version are free, with no ads and no account needed.

Which devices does it support?

Phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or later. Some checks depend on the hardware and Android version. When a check isn't available on your device, the app says so instead of marking it as failed.

Can I use it before buying or selling a used phone?

Yes, that's what it's built for. Run a Full Device Check and save the report as a PDF to share with the buyer or seller. Each report has a unique ID.

Does it tell me what my phone is worth?

No. Dignovos gives a condition grade from A to D, but not a price. A fair price also depends on the exact model, storage size, region and cosmetic condition, and the app has none of that data.

Why doesn't it show my battery health percentage?

Android doesn't give apps the battery's original or current capacity, so any percentage would be a guess. Dignovos shows the data Android does provide, such as temperature, voltage, charging behaviour, the Android health status, and the cycle count on Android 14+ where the phone reports it.

Does it need root?

No. Dignovos uses only standard Android features. It never uses root, hidden system features or workarounds.
